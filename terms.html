Your right to cancel

If you're buying as a consumer in the EU, you normally have a 14-day right to cancel an online purchase and receive a full refund, without giving a reason.

Both of our products, though, are the kind of purchase the law treats differently: the wishes booklet is digital content delivered immediately, and a memorial page is a service that begins as soon as you start using the builder. In both cases, the law allows that 14-day right to be lost once delivery or performance genuinely begins — but only if we've first asked for, and you've given, your explicit consent to start immediately and your acknowledgment that you're giving up the cancellation right by doing so. That consent has to be collected at the point of purchase, not buried in a page like this one.

What this means in practice: until that consent step exists in the checkout flow, this exception doesn't validly apply, and a buyer could still have a 14-day statutory cancellation right regardless of what's written here. This is flagged above as something to fix before launch, not something these words alone can settle.
