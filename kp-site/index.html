Questions

A few things people ask

Is this only for after my pet has died?

No — most people start the booklet while their pet is healthy, simply to have it ready.

Can I make the memorial page private?

Yes. You choose public, unlisted, or fully private — and you can change it at any time.

Does the booklet replace advice from my vet?

No. It's a planning guide, not medical or legal advice — we always encourage a conversation with your vet.

Is there a subscription for the memorial page?

No. A memorial page is €29, paid once — no renewals, and nothing that lapses. If Kindred Paws ever ceases operating, every page is kept at the same link for at least three years rather than deleted.

Can I add a life celebration later?

Yes, at any time — before or after the memorial page is already live.
