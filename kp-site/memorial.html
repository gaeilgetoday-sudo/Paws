Questions

Before you start

Do I have to finish it in one go?

No. Once you've paid, you can come back and keep working on it whenever you're ready — and nothing is visible to anyone until you choose to publish. There's no rush and no deadline.

Who will be able to see it?

By default, only people you send the link to. You can also make it fully public, or keep it entirely private and visible only to you. You can change your mind at any point, before or after publishing.

Can I stop people leaving messages?

Yes. The guestbook can be switched off entirely, and when it's on, nothing appears until you've read and approved it. Candles are anonymous and carry no message, so there's nothing there to misuse.

What if I already have the wishes booklet?

Then most of the hard thinking is already done — their story, their favourite things, what you'd want people to remember. You can copy those answers straight across.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — if you change your mind before publishing the page, email us and we'll refund you in full, no questions asked.
